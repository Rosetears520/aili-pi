### Lesson 42 — Multi-Logo Page

#### Search Methods

1. Search the organization's official website.
2. Search for `company + LOGO + PNG` and sort from large to small.
3. Search an app store with `software name + App Store`.
4. Use a dedicated logo site.

#### Two Cautions

1. Align logos visually, not only geometrically.
2. Do not turn every logo white by simply increasing brightness; doing so may remove identifying details. Prefer an official white logo where available.

#### Four Layout Ideas

1. Place logos inside consistent supporting shapes.
2. Offset sizes deliberately.
3. Build a logo cloud.
4. Use controlled 3D rotation.

### Lesson 26 — Business-Style Section Divider

#### Divider Methods

1. Differentiate divider slides from other slides, for example by using a dark divider in an otherwise light deck.
2. Enlarge the section number and use a more expressive display face such as Helvetica, Montserrat, Facon, LIBRARY3AM, or BLACKHAWK when available and appropriate.
3. Fill the number with imagery:
   - **Intersect:** keep only the overlap.
   - **Subtract:** reveal the image behind the text.
   - **Slide-background fill:** set the image as the slide background, convert the text to shapes, and apply Slide Background Fill. This keeps the text freely movable.
   - **Picture/texture fill:** cut the image and fill the text with it. Tiling can reduce distortion, although positioning and scale remain harder to control.
   - **Local highlight:** duplicate a light vectorized text shape, edit its points until only the target stroke remains, and change its color or brightness.

#### Inner Shadow

1. Use an inner-shadow preset to strengthen depth.
2. Use a white inner shadow on a background-colored shape to simulate inner glow.
3. Use a white inner shadow to simulate thickness.

#### Technology-Style Broken Lines

1. Draw broken lines with Freeform.
2. Choose an angular typeface.
3. To crop lines through text, duplicate/vectorize the text and use an intersection operation with the line.

### Lesson 40 — Western-Style Medical Team Page

#### Process Images

1. Unify backgrounds with transparent-color removal, background removal, or an approved cutout method.
2. Use three horizontal guides to equalize face size.
3. Use one crop shape for every portrait; select the image first and the shape second, and then Intersect.
   - To let the head break outside the frame, keep a duplicate image/shape pair and place the extracted head over the original.
   - Alternatively, extend the crop shape upward with a rectangle, combine the shapes, intersect with the image, and place the result over the base frame.

#### Unify the Style

1. Use the same decorative elements.
2. Use the same text formatting.

#### Layout

- Use a parallel arrangement with deliberate vertical staggering.

### Lesson 32 — Data-Chart Page

#### Order Pie Slices

1. Follow clockwise reading order from the top.
2. Place the largest segment immediately to the right of 12 o'clock, then order the remaining segments by size.

#### Color

1. **Lightness gradient:** generate variants from the theme color, calculate brightness with temporary shapes, or use an approved color-scale tool. The source note names [Eva Design System](https://colors.eva.design/).
2. **Analogous colors:** use an analogous-color reference and keep the range controlled. The source note names [Adobe Color](https://color.adobe.com/).

`[UNVERIFIED]` Availability, licensing, and current behavior of the named external tools were not checked during this translation.

#### Data Labels

1. Place them above.
2. Place them around the chart.
3. Curve them around the form where appropriate.

#### Advanced Treatments

1. Add a larger semi-transparent black ring in the center for subtle depth.
2. Add a separate curve over a chart and use round line caps to simulate rounded corners.
3. For a 3D offset, use a small perspective value, duplicate the chart, place the copy above, and remove fill from the lower section.

### Lesson 53 — Fresh Business Combination Chart

1. Insert a combination chart and combine a line chart with an area chart using the same data.
2. Add labels through the chart controls and place them above the data where appropriate.
3. Enable a smooth line for the line series.

#### Create a Gradient Area Shape

1. Delete or duplicate the area series as needed.
2. Paste a copy as an Enhanced Metafile.
3. Align it with the original chart.
4. Ungroup twice.
5. Insert a rectangle no wider than the curve and keep a copy of the line.
6. Select the rectangle and then the line, and apply Fragment.
7. Delete unwanted fragments and combine the remaining area.
8. Apply the gradient.

### Lesson 11 — Academic Card-Style Data Chart

#### Select a Chart

- Compare magnitude with a column/bar chart.
- Show part-to-whole composition with a pie/doughnut chart.
- Show change over time with a line chart.

**Visual description — relationship-to-chart catalog:** A dark orange-and-black infographic groups chart types into three rows. “Comparison” includes column, bar, line, and radar examples. “Composition” includes pie, stacked column, and stacked area examples. “Relationship/Distribution” includes scatter, bubble, histogram, and normal-distribution examples.

**Visual description — three chart cards:** Three large rounded dark cards show a column chart, doughnut chart, and line chart. Small labels under the column chart list composition, comparison, proportion, distribution, and ranking. The doughnut card lists composition and proportion. The line card lists trend, comparison, and time.

#### Column-Chart Optimization

1. Control the gap width.
2. Weaken secondary elements.
3. Emphasize the important data.

#### Pie-Chart Optimization

1. Integrate labels with the chart instead of relying on a remote legend.
2. Keep the colors simple and consistent.
3. Sort from small to large when that ordering supports the reading task.
4. Point label leader lines toward the center of the pie.

#### Balance the Page

1. Combine a line chart with an area chart.
2. Use a restrained grid.

#### Line-Chart Optimization

1. Weaken secondary elements.
2. Smooth the line where appropriate.
3. Increase the visual area of the data display.
